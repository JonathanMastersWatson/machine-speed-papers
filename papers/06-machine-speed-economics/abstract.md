# Abstract — Machine-Speed Economics

An economy is buyers meeting sellers, prices moving, and obligations settling — with enough reliability that strangers will trade. This paper describes that economy with the humans removed from the middle of each transaction, and argues that what makes it possible is not faster money but independently witnessed evidence. The paper makes six claims. First, the machine-speed economy requires nothing that does not already exist. Agents carry a purse — bounded spending authority committable at the point of action — and a mandate — preconstrained limits on what may be done, for whom, at what cost. Every component maps to deployed technology. The assembly is connection, not invention. Second, admissibility becomes the matching criterion. Today matching happens first and compliance is checked afterward, usually by humans, usually too late. Where two agents' constraint sets intersect, a trade is admissible; where they do not, no candidate ever appears. There is no non-compliant transaction to prevent because it was never matchable. Third, once latency, uptime, jurisdiction, security posture and energy mix are attested by parties with no position in the trade, those attestations become bid attributes. Price discovery turns differences in the real world into differences in the number. The market prices pickiness, which no human market can sustain, because no human holds the whole vector. Fourth, a receipt is not a payment. Execution, evidence, obligation, settlement and enforcement are five distinct functions, and collapsing any two destroys the unpaid state — the economy's ability to say the service occurred, the obligation exists, the payment has not cleared. An architecture that fires a payment per action has nothing to net. Fifth, and this is the paper's candidate for what is new: a single evidence artifact does three jobs at once. The receipt that proves a determination was legitimate is the IOU the settlement layer nets and the input the valuation layer prices on. Governance control, settlement primitive, valuation denominator — one artifact, three functions, each reinforcing the others. No prior literature appears to synthesize them. Sixth, the thesis is falsifiable by a single measurement. Run the same market governed and ungoverned and compare prices. If the spread is zero — if nobody will pay for attested terms, deterministic admissibility and independent evidence — the thesis is wrong. Not incomplete. Wrong. Participation is voluntary throughout. No regulator mandates the gates; no treaty compels the witness. The enforcement is access. Preliminaries Vocabulary. This paper uses the terminology established across Papers 1–5: execution boundary, Executable Governance Kernel (EGK), admissibility, independent witness, policy compilation, Compiled Region, execution surface. It names no implementation of any of them. Naming. To avoid collision with the EGK, this paper does not use the abbreviation EKG for the continuous economic trace described in Chapter 14.
